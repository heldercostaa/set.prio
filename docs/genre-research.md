# Genre vocabulary research

Verified November 12, 2026. Primary and secondary classifications use only the twelve choices in `src/data/genres.ts`. Unsupported older subgenre labels were removed. These are subjective listening guides, with the warning retained in the interface.

| Artist           | Estimate           | Evidence                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ---------------- | ------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Alan Walker      | Big room           | [Official promoter event taxonomy](https://www.insomniac.com/events/alan-walker-2026-09-06-san-jose-ca/) includes Big Room.                                                       |
| Due              | House; Techno      | [House Mag festival artist profile](https://housemag.com.br/o-que-esperar-dos-djs-brasileiros-no-tomorrowland-brasil-2027-pt-1/) describes both styles.                           |
| ETTA             | House; Tech house  | [Artist-supplied Beatport biography](https://www.beatport.com/artist/etta/127150) describes the Brazilian sisters and their sound.                                                |
| Fezzo            | Tech house; Techno | [Brazilian duo's Beatport biography](https://www.beatport.com/artist/fezzo/336304) explicitly describes these influences. The German solo artist with the same name was excluded. |
| Marian           | House              | [Official promoter profile for Marian (BR)](https://www.insomniac.com/music/artists/marian-br/) explicitly labels House.                                                          |
| The Chainsmokers | House              | [Official promoter profile](https://www.insomniac.com/music/artists/the-chainsmokers/) includes House alongside Electronic and Pop.                                               |

Missing classifications are intentional where the research does not support a permitted style. [Ray Volpe](https://www.insomniac.com/music/artists/ray-volpe/) is labeled Dubstep by his promoter, outside this vocabulary. [NGHTMRE](https://www.insomniac.com/music/artists/nghtmre/), [Illenium](https://www.insomniac.com/music/artists/illenium/), and [R3HAB](https://www.insomniac.com/music/artists/r3hab/) have broader Electronic labels; these do not justify selecting one of the narrower permitted styles. Pop acts, an MC, unidentified surprise acts, and unsupported bass classifications remain unknown rather than being forced into House or Techno. Their verified recordings remain available.
